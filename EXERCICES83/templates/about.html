My course

My name is Amina Zabona, and I am a web developer with a degree in Applied Computer Science for Business Management. Passionate about digital technologies, computer science, and creating innovative solutions, I enjoy exploring the possibilities offered by web development to design tools that address real needs and make users’ daily lives easier. My academic background and various training experiences have enabled me to gain expertise in programming, application design, and database management. I am particularly interested in web development, user interface design, and the creation of interactive applications. I place great importance on code quality, project organization, user experience, and the adaptability of applications across different devices.

I work with a variety of technologies and tools, including HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, JavaScript, Python, and Flask. These technologies enable me to design modern interfaces, develop interactive features, implement server-side logic, and connect applications to databases. I’m also interested in full-stack development, REST APIs, data management, and new technologies that are driving innovation in the IT sector.
